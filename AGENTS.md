# BIM DASHBOARD DEVELOPMENT DIRECTIVE & WORKSPACE RULES

## ZERO AI SLOP DIRECTIVE (MANDATORY)

Under NO circumstances should any AI assistant or developer reproduce the stereotypical "AI-generated SaaS dashboard":
1. **NO 3-COLUMN DASHBOARD CARDS:** Never squeeze a 3D CAD/BIM viewport into a center card between two sidebars, with 2D Recharts stacked underneath and 2x2 bubbly KPI cards on the right.
2. **NO TRAFFIC LIGHT BADGE SOUP:** Never clutter the top bar with adjacent primary blue, lime green, and amber pill badges.
3. **MANDATORY DESIGN STANDARD:** Swiss Architectural & Monolith CAD Workspace (Autodesk Forma / Rhino / Twinmotion / Blender):
   - **Full-Bleed 3D Viewport:** The 3D canvas is the hero, taking 100% of the workspace stage.
   - **Floating Telemetry HUD:** High-density live telemetry floats in a subtle bottom-left HUD chip directly over the canvas.
   - **Unified Right Inspector:** A single 320px high-density property panel with segmented tabs (`Envelope`, `Climate`, `Specs`, `Elements`).
   - **Collapsible Bottom Drawer:** 2D analytical charts live in a collapsible bottom drawer, toggleable with a clean drawer tab.
   - **Monochrome & Monolith Palette:** Neutral graphite slate (`#0e1117`, `#161a22`, `#21262d`), subtle hairline borders (`rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`), and high-contrast typography (Inter / Geist / JetBrains Mono).
